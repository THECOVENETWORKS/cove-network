## Color & Style Preview

**Header Navigation Bar**
- Background: White
- Border: Light gray (#ddd)
- Text: Dark gray (#333)
- CTA Button: Orange (#f39c12)

**Hero Section**
- Background: Blue gradient (#2c5aa0 to #1e3a8a)
- Text: White
- CTA Buttons: Orange primary, white outline secondary

**Cards**
- Background: White or light gray (#f8f9fa)
- Border: Light gray (#ddd) or 4px solid orange left border
- Hover: Box shadow, slight lift animation

**Buttons**
- Primary: Orange (#f39c12) → Dark orange on hover (#e67e22)
- Secondary: Transparent with blue border → Blue fill on hover

**Text**
- Headlines: Dark gray (#2c3e50), bold
- Body: Medium gray (#555)
- Links: Blue (#2c5aa0) → Orange on hover

## What Makes This Design Special

✨ **Clean & Minimal**
- No cluttered layouts
- Plenty of whitespace
- Clear hierarchy

👥 **Human-Focused**
- Real community feel (not corporate)
- Authentic tone
- Emphasis on relationships

📱 **Mobile-Perfect**
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Readable on small screens
- Fast loading

🎯 **Trust-Building**
- Clear contact information
- Easy member discovery
- Transparent information

## Next: How to View the Live Site

Once you upload to IONOS hosting:

1. Visit: `https://covenetwork.co.uk`
2. You'll see the homepage exactly as shown above
3. Click through all pages to experience the flow
4. Test search/filter on directory
5. Click a member profile to see the shareable link

To preview locally before uploading:
1. Download all files from GitHub
2. Open `index.html` in your web browser
3. Navigate through all pages
4. (Note: Directory won't show members until Airtable is connected)
